# 2026-11-21 · 4 min · process, ecology

Every few months I archive a side project into a folder called compost/. It is not a graveyard — nothing is buried. Ideas break down and feed the next thing.

The rule

No commits in six months, no user mail in three: it moves to compost with a one-paragraph autopsy — what it was, what it taught me, what survived.
